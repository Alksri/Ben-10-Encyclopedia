The concept

A database that behaves like a device. Every interaction should feel like the watch responding to a wrist.

The visual direction is “Plumber field terminal”: near-black surfaces, acid-green signal, sharp panels, mono telemetry labels and one loud accent per alien. The Omnitrix hourglass is the only logo. Type is chunky display, functional mono, readable body.

Motion is functional, not decorative. Internal links trigger a warp overlay with the destination form’s name. Alien pages arrive with a transformation sequence. Cards expose their stats on hover. The 404 glitches because the signal is lost — not because glitch is trendy.

Everything is progressive: the site works from the filesystem, search handles typos with Levenshtein matching, and the whole motion layer disables under prefers-reduced-motion.
